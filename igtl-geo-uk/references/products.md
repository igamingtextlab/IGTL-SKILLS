# Products, bonuses and promotions

Read this file when the text describes slots, other casino games, live casino, poker, sports betting or bingo, and also bonuses, free spins, free bets, wagering and any promotion, including the full promotion journey. Standalone content about lotteries is out of scope; a lottery counts here only in the mixed-product ban.

The British regulatory requirements are described below. Check the requirements and the actual features of the specific operator separately. For an operator with a confirmed Gambling Commission licence, take into account the product and the British version. If the licence is not confirmed, describe verified features as facts, without statements about their lawfulness in GB (`licensing.md`, section 5). Do not attribute limits or protection mechanisms to an operator just because they are listed here.

Sources and effective dates are in `sources.md`.

## Online slots (Casino, Remote)

- **Naming:** in GB the word "casino" is acceptable for a slots offer. Do not carry over the German market's restriction on "Casino".
- **What counts as online slots** [Requirement]: under the 2025 regulations, a casino game that is played online and is reel-based: moving or changing images or text are displayed as reels or by other means, and a win is determined by the resulting arrangement. The operator's classification is a useful source [Fact], but if in doubt, check the game's mechanics (Slingo, some instant win games and the like) against this definition. If it is ambiguous, tell the task owner and do not state whether the limit applies.
- **Maximum stake** [Requirement]: per game cycle of an online slot, **£5** for players aged 25 and over and **£2** for players aged 18 to 24. The £5 limit has applied to all adults since 9 April 2025; the lower £2 limit for ages 18 to 24 since 21 May 2025. The limit covers the whole amount staked in the game cycle and applies only to online slots; it does not extend to roulette, blackjack or other casino games.
  * Write: "maximum stake of £5 per spin (£2 if you are aged 18 to 24)".
  * Do not promise stakes above £5 on slots, "high roller slots" or a VIP increase of the limit.
  * Distinguish the statutory stake limit, the maximum bet while wagering a bonus and the player's deposit limit. A bonus cap does not raise the stake limit. If the article gives different values, explain how they relate.
- **Game design** [Requirement; for slots since 31 October 2021]. Do not describe the following for a licensee's slots:
  * autoplay: RTS 8A;
  * features that shorten the time to the result, such as turbo, quick spin, slam stop: RTS 14E. Exception: bonus and feature games where no additional stake is made; speeding them up is not banned by this rule;
  * a game cycle shorter than **2.5 seconds**, and starting the next spin by holding a button: RTS 14D;
  * simultaneous play of several games (split screen, multi-screen): RTS 14C;
  * celebrating a return that is less than or equal to the stake (losses disguised as wins): RTS 14F.
  During a session the operator shows the net position (RTS 2E) and the time played (RTS 13C). If you find a feature from the list above at the operator, report the discrepancy.
- **Bonus Buy (feature buy-in)** [per Gambling Commission guidance]: in January 2020 the Gambling Commission publicly warned licensees about feature buy-ins, citing RTS 3A and 14A; the six operators it contacted removed the feature. The RTS contain no ban by name: do not write "banned by law". Describe it only as it actually exists in the British version of the game at the specific operator [Fact]. Do not carry Bonus Buy over from a description of the version for other markets. If a licensee has it in GB, report the discrepancy.
- **Jackpots, gamble feature** [Fact]: progressive jackpots are offered by licensees in GB; describe them as they exist at the operator. Do not call a gamble feature banned or permitted on the strength of its name; describe it as it exists in the British version.
- **RTP** [Fact]: a slot can have several RTP versions. Give the RTP of the version the specific operator offers, and its source (the game information at the operator). If only the provider's value is known: "RTP according to the provider: [X]%; the version offered by [Operator] may differ". No confirmation: a `[VERIFY-n]` marker and an item "RTP and game version" in the report.
- **Demo play** [Requirement: LCCP SR 3.2.11]: a licensee verifies the customer's age before giving access to free-to-play versions of gambling games on its site. For a licensee's site, do not write "play the demo without registering".

## Other casino games (roulette, blackjack, live casino, game shows)

- There is no statutory stake limit. Table limits come from the operator [Fact].
- Since 17 January 2025 the slots requirements have been extended to other casino games [Requirement]:
  * a minimum game cycle of **5 seconds**: RTS 14G (does not apply to slots, which have 2.5 seconds, or to peer-to-peer poker);
  * a ban on autoplay for all online games, including bingo: RTS 8A;
  * a ban on turbo and slam stop: RTS 14E, with the same exception for bonus games with no additional stake;
  * a ban on simultaneous play of several games: RTS 14C (except peer-to-peer poker);
  * a ban on celebrating a loss as a win: RTS 14F;
  * display of the net position: RTS 2E (except peer-to-peer poker).
- "Reveal all" and "scratch all" features, and games where the stake is lost unless the player acts (crash games), are not covered by the ban on speeding up [per RTS 14E]. Describe them as they are.
- **Applicability by category** [iGTL Standard]: apply the game cycle requirement and the other RTS 14 rules to each game according to its category. Slots: 2.5 seconds (RTS 14D). Other casino games, except peer-to-peer poker: 5 seconds (RTS 14G). For Slingo, instant win, crash, scratch games and game shows, establish the category from the operator's classification and the mechanics (definition of online slots above). Instant win exists both as a casino game and as a lottery product (instant win lotteries), to which casino rules do not apply. Until a game's category is established, do not attribute a specific minimum cycle to it. Listing several game types in one paragraph does not mean one requirement applies to all of them.
- In live casino, a re-bet feature, where the player repeats the bet manually for each round, is acceptable in place of autoplay [per Gambling Commission guidance].

## Poker

- Do not carry the slots rules (the £5/£2 limit, 2.5 seconds) over to poker.
- Peer-to-peer poker is exempt from the 5-second cycle (RTS 14G), the ban on simultaneous play (RTS 14C) and the net position display (RTS 2E). Multi-table play is possible; how many tables are available comes from the operator [Fact]. Automatic posting of blinds in peer-to-peer poker is not caught by the autoplay ban (RTS 8A).
- Poker against the house (Casino Hold'em, video poker) is a casino game, and the 5-second rule applies to it.
- Do not promise automatic re-buys or chip top-ups at the table [per RTS 14A: top-ups only at the player's decision].
- Limits, formats and rules on bots come from the operator's T&Cs [Fact].

## Sports betting (General Betting, Remote)

- British regulation does not impose special restrictions on live markets of the kind found in Germany. Describe only the markets the operator actually offers [Fact].
- Promotions combining betting and casino in one offer are banned (see "Bonuses and promotions"): "Bet £10, get 50 free spins" is not allowed from 19 January 2026.
- Price boosts and enhanced odds: if part of the winnings is paid in free bets, that is a significant condition and must be shown next to the offer [Requirement: CAP Code 8.17].
- Account restrictions: do not promise that the operator does not restrict winning players unless the operator itself says so [Fact].
- Cash out, each way, acca insurance: according to the operator's terms [Fact].
- Do not use the operator's taxes and levies to explain odds or margin without confirmation (`player-protection.md`, "Taxes").

## Bingo

- Bingo (Remote) is a separate activity in the licence. Bingo, casino, betting and lottery are four different product types for the mixed-product ban: "deposit on bingo, get free spins on slots" is not allowed from 19 January 2026 [Requirement].
- Autoplay in online bingo has been banned since 17 January 2025 [Requirement: RTS 8A]. Per Gambling Commission guidance, the ban does not cover auto-daub (automatically marking numbers in a game the player has already entered) or buying tickets for scheduled games. Describe these features as they exist at the operator.

## Game catalogue and providers [iGTL Standard]

- The number of games, the availability of individual titles and the list of providers are dynamic facts [Fact]. Take them from the British lobby on the date of the check. Provider pages, the .com version of the site and third-party reviews do not confirm the British catalogue.
- A game available in other countries may be missing from the British version or run in a different version (RTP, features). Check separately whether the game is available at the operator in GB.
- Give the number of games in a form you can confirm: an exact number only if you counted or the operator states it; otherwise "more than [X]" with a confirmed lower bound. Do not present an approximate value as exact.

## Casino and Sportsbook: terms do not carry over [iGTL Standard]

At the same brand, the casino and the sportsbook usually have different welcome offers, bonus terms, section T&Cs, payment method exclusions and apps. In the licence they are different activities (`licensing.md`).

- Tag each term in the fact table with its product in the Scope field.
- Do not carry one product's terms over to the other: casino bonus wagering and free bet minimum odds, time limits, method exclusions, limits.
- Describe account-level features (verification, deposit limits, self-exclusion) as shared only if the operator says so or it follows from a requirement.
- If a source does not say which product a term applies to, it does not confirm it for both: check the T&Cs of the relevant section or use `[VERIFY-n]`.

## Bonuses and promotions

- **Wagering requirements** [Requirement: LCCP SR 5.1.1, from 19 January 2026]: a licensee cannot require bonus funds to be played through more than **10 times**. Going by the Gambling Commission's example (a £10 bonus with 50x wagering meant £500 of bets), the multiplier applies to bonus funds: at 10x, a £10 bonus requires no more than £100 of bets.
  * If winnings from free spins or a free bet are credited as bonus funds with wagering, the same cap applies in the spirit of the rule. The Gambling Commission does not address this case specifically.
  * If the operator calculates wagering on the deposit plus bonus or on another basis, state the basis exactly as in the terms and tell the task owner about a possible conflict with the 10x cap.
  * The rule does not ban game weighting; describe each category's contribution as in the terms. If slots count 100% and roulette 10%, the actual amount of play on roulette is higher.
- **Mixed-product promotions** [Requirement: LCCP SR 5.1.1, from 19 January 2026]: one incentive cannot combine different product types (betting, casino, bingo, lottery). Per Gambling Commission guidance:
  * acceptable: "Bet £5 and get a £10 free bet"; "Spend £5 on casino products and get 20 free spins"; "Bet £5 to get £10 credit to use on all products", where the player chooses the product for the bonus;
  * not acceptable: "Bet £5 and get 20 free spins"; "Bet £5 and get a £5 free bet and 20 free spins"; "Spend £10 on any product and get a £5 free bet", where the operator itself restricts the reward to one product.
  Excluding individual games within a category (for example, roulette or instant win) is acceptable if the terms state it clearly. If you find a mixed-product promotion at a licensee, report the discrepancy and do not promote it in the text.
- **Promotion terms** [Requirement: LCCP SR 5.1.1]: terms must be clear, transparent and fair and available before taking part.
- **Significant conditions** [Requirement: CAP Code 8.17; CAP guidance on free bets and bonuses]: minimum deposit, wagering, time limit, eligible games and markets, minimum odds, maximum bet while wagering, withdrawal cap, excluded payment methods, whether the free bet stake is returned: show them next to the offer. The full terms are at most one click away.
- **"Free", "risk-free", "money back"** [Requirement: CAP Code 3.23; CAP guidance on free bets and bonuses]: "free" only if the terms do not contradict the word; "risk-free" only if the player bears no loss; "money back" means cash; a refund in bonuses does not qualify.
- **"No wagering"**: only if the terms confirm it [Fact].
- **Current offer check** [iGTL Standard]. Describe as available now only a running British offer. Before using an offer, establish its type:
  * **running**: in the British T&Cs or on the promotions page on the date of the check, and the period has not expired;
  * **ended**: the period in the terms has passed, or the page says "offer ended" or similar; the direct URL may still open;
  * **archived**: an old version of the terms, an archived page, a cached copy or a third-party account of past terms;
  * **test or personalised**: a version for some users, by invitation or for a specific affiliate code; its availability to everyone is not confirmed;
  * **regional**: for another country, or the generic .com version.
  Signs that a URL leads to an offer that is not running: dates in the terms that have passed, the promotion missing from the promotions page, a conflict with the current general bonus terms. An offer that is not running is not described in the Content Draft as available; if it cannot be left out, give its period in the past tense. Give a validity period only if it is confirmed; if there is no end date, do not invent one and do not write "ongoing" without a basis.
- **Promotion minimum deposit** [iGTL Standard]: a term of that promotion. The minimum deposit and limits for payment methods come from the cashier (`player-protection.md`, "Payment methods"); do not extend the bonus minimum to the methods.

## Full promotion journey [iGTL Standard]

Check this when the text describes a specific promotion: welcome offer, free spins, free bet, reload, cashback. Source: the full terms of that promotion and the operator's general bonus terms [Fact].

**Stages.** For each stage, record in the fact table the actions the player must take, the time limit and the event the time limit runs from:

| Stage | What to check |
| :---- | :---- |
| 1. Claiming and activation | Registration, opt-in, promo code, manual claim in the promotions section, qualifying deposit or bet, excluded payment methods; the time limit for claiming and its start event (registration, deposit, crediting) |
| 2. Using the bonus or free spins | Games, spin value, maximum bet; the time limit for use and its start event (crediting, activation, first launch of the game); what happens to unused free spins |
| 3. Meeting the withdrawal conditions | Wagering and its basis, game contribution, the wagering period and its start event, withdrawal cap, the order in which cash and bonus funds are used |

Several time limits of one promotion, for example 7 days to claim and 30 days to wager, are not merged into one and are not carried over from one stage to another.

**Also check the terms on:**
- manually claiming the bonus or free spins (claim, opt-in);
- using all free spins and what happens to the rest;
- the order in which cash and bonus funds are used: which is staked first and how that affects withdrawal;
- a special registration page, promo code or other mandatory route to the offer.

A term not found in the full terms is no proof that it does not exist. If the promotion journey cannot be described correctly without it, use a `[VERIFY-n]` marker; otherwise do not state either that it exists or that it does not.

**Link to the full terms.** A direct link to the full terms of each promotion described sits in the published part of the text, next to the promotion. A list of sources in unpublished notes to the editor does not replace it. This is the editorial way to meet the CAP requirement that the full terms are at most one click away ("Bonuses and promotions" above). If the link is unknown: a `[VERIFY-n]` marker and an item "link to the full promotion terms" in the report.
