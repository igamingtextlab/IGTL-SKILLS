What the skill checks

  • No British licence is attributed to an operator without an entry in the Gambling Commission register.
  • Product rules: the slots stake limit of £5 (£2 for players aged 18 to 24), game design requirements, bonus wagering no higher than 10x, the mixed-product ban.
  • Player protection: age and identity checks, financial checks, deposit limits, self-exclusion and GAMSTOP.
  • No credit cards or anonymous payments offered at British licensees.
  • CAP Code advertising rules: no appeal to minors, no promises of winning, no pressure to play immediately.
  • British English and British formats for amounts, dates, times and odds; current help contacts.
  • Disputed, outdated or unverified data is never turned into a confident statement — it goes to the editor as UNVERIFIED.

What you get

  • Content Draft — clean text with no internal comments.
  • Research & QA Report — status of the piece, verified facts with sources, source conflicts, unverified data and manual checks.

Great Britain only (England, Scotland, Wales). The skill does not replace a lawyer and does not assess whether an offer or campaign is lawful.
