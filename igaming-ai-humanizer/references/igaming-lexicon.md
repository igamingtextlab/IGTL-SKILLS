## Categories (with examples)

1. Evaluative adjectives: high-quality, premium, top-notch, excellent
   → "high-quality gaming experience"
2. Unsupported positive claims: exciting, thrilling, amazing, incredible,
   fantastic → "an exciting range of games"
3. Clichés: state-of-the-art, cutting-edge, next-level → "cutting-edge
   gaming technology"
4. Templated feature descriptions: user-friendly interface, easy to
   navigate, seamless experience → "a user-friendly interface that makes it
   easy to navigate"
5. Marketing repetition: unbeatable bonuses, generous promotions, lucrative
   rewards → "unbeatable bonuses and generous promotions"
6. Overstated benefits: maximize your winnings, boost your chances,
   increase your rewards → "maximize your winnings with our exclusive
   bonuses"
7. Vague blanket claims: something for everyone, suitable for all types of
   players, a game for every taste → "a game for every taste and
   preference"
8. Overuse of "experience": gaming experience, unforgettable experience,
   unique experience → "an unforgettable gaming experience"
9. Unsupported comparisons: better than other casinos, stands out from the
   rest, leading in the industry → "better than other casinos in the
   market"
10. Luck/winning promises: win big, hit the jackpot, luck is on your side
    → "win big with our progressive jackpots"
