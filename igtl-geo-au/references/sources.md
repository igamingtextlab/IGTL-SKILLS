# Rule register and updating the skill

Read this file in any mode before relying on a legal rule to state anything, correct, delete or make a remark (`SKILL.md`, section 3), and to cite a rule, check its commencement date or start of application, and update the skill.

**Priority.** The wording in the reference files is a saved summary of the rules as of the check date. If the current primary source differs from it, the primary source prevails; tell the person who set the task about the discrepancy.

**Columns.** "Checked" is the date the source was last read during skill maintenance. Facts about a specific operator are checked separately in every task.

## Verification statuses

The register uses only the five statuses in this table. A check against a primary source may be done by the package maintainer or an iGTL reviewer. If the check was not done by the maintenance tool, the source column states who checked, when and exactly which provisions were confirmed; the availability of a document is not in itself a check. The "Status" column contains exactly one value, with no qualifiers in brackets; explanations (which page did not open, where a copy of the document is) go in the source column. If a rule rests on sources of different strength, it is split into rows, and each gets its own status.

| Status | Meaning | May be relied on without rechecking |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Primary source | The official text of the rule or the official page has been read; the provision the skill's rule rests on has been checked against a quote of the key passage | Yes |
| Primary source (guidance) | An official explanation, guide, notice or decision of a regulator, department or court has been read; it does not create a rule of its own | Yes, with the attribution "per [body] guidance" |
| ⚠ Primary source (paraphrase) | The official text has been read, but the key wording has not been checked word for word: the provision number and general meaning are known | No |
| ⚠ Primary source (excerpt) | The official page did not open in full; checked against an excerpt of it in search results or a quote in another document | No |
| ⚠ Secondary source | Law firms, industry press, a copy of a document on a third-party site, one operator's terms as an example | No |

How to use ⚠ rows before they are rechecked: the section "⚠ norms: limits of use" below.

**Check of application dates, 23.11.2026.**
- Basis: s 2 (commencement table) and Schedule 5, items 12A–12E of Act No. 72, 2026; s 62ZZC(1) for the Opt-out Register.
- What was checked: a search of legislation.gov.au and the web for a notifiable instrument of the Minister under items 12A, 12B, 12D, 12E and a Proclamation under s 62ZZC(1). The Interactions tab of Act C2026A00072 on legislation.gov.au is not accessible to the tool.
- Result: the setting of the day the application of s 62ZQ, 62ZR, 62ZS, 62ZN(2)–(4), 62ZSA starts and the publication of the Proclamation were not confirmed at the check: the tool's search did not find them, and the Interactions tab could not be opened. This is not enough to state that the date has not been set. The provisions commence on 01.01.2027; the commission ban (s 62ZT) applies from 01.01.2027.
- What to do in a task: this is a dynamic norm (section "Dynamic norms"). Before relying on it, check the Act's Interactions tab on legislation.gov.au. Give any date found with the name of the instrument. If the check fails, write in the report "setting of the date not confirmed at the check on [date]; the skill's last check: 23.11.2026", in the text "applies from a day the Minister sets by notifiable instrument", and do not write that the ban on direct marketing of inducements applies from 01.01.2027.

## ⚠ norms: limits of use

Until rechecked against the primary source, each of these rows may be used only as set out below. None of them may be the only basis for a statement about the law, a correction, a deletion or a remark about a breach (`SKILL.md`, section 3). If such a rule is the only thing pointing to a problem, the only permitted output is a source check (what to verify and in which official source), without a conclusion of breach. A question about how a rule applies to a specific platform, offer or payment scheme is for compliance or a lawyer.

| Register row | Allowed before rechecking | Not allowed |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| ACMA register columns, old register page | guidance on what to look for in an entry; the ACMA register or the licensing authority's official register is opened at every operator check, and the result rests on the register actually opened | describing a register entry without opening it in the task |
| Online bet after a race starts | a manual check item in the report | writing that such bets are permitted or prohibited |
| "PayID pokies" | mentioning the AUSTRAC warning in the report as context | stating in the text that a specific operator uses such a scheme |
| Schedule 2: "published in Australia", removal notices, payment blocking | a risk item in the report for offshore texts | describing the procedure and obligations as established |
| Trade promotions | a manual check item | describing trade promotion rules in the text |
| Departmental guidance on preparatory work | an additional reference next to Schedule 5 | replacing Schedule 5 with it as the basis for the date |
| Commissions and affiliate payments | noting in the report that s 62ZT may affect the payment scheme, the decision is for a lawyer | assessing a specific affiliate's payment scheme |
| Promotion of odds | a question in manual checks | writing about a ban or permission for websites |
| Lowering a deposit limit immediately | writing it as a [Fact] of a specific operator under its terms | presenting it as a legal requirement |
| Lotteries and keno outside the credit ban; review of the credit ban | context in the report | statements in the text about lotteries (out of scope) and the outcome of the review |

## How to update the skill

Section for the package maintainer. The release procedure for public versions is kept by iGTL outside the package.

1. Before releasing a new version and after key dates, check: ACMA pages (Changes to the Act in 2026, guidance on triple lock, notable persons, inducements, the opt-out register, blocking notices, the register, investigations); the IGA compilation and the Interactions tab of Act C2026A00072 on legislation.gov.au (notifiable instruments under Schedule 5, items 12A, 12B, 12D, 12E; Proclamation under s 62ZZC(1)); state regulator websites (Liquor & Gaming NSW, WA, VGCCC, CBS SA, Northern Territory Wagering Commission); AUSTRAC; DSS on the NCPF; the outcome of the credit ban review.
2. Key dates for an extra check: 01.01.2027; publication of any notifiable instrument under Schedule 5; the Proclamation starting the Opt-out Register; final ACMA guidance replacing initial guidance. After each check, update the "Dynamic norms" table.
3. For a rule that has changed, update the register row with a direct URL, then the reference file text and, if the rule is in section 6 of `SKILL.md`, that section.
4. Recheck ⚠ rows against the primary source at the first opportunity. Raise a status only after reading a suitable primary source; put the reading date in the "Checked" column.
5. After 01.01.2027, remove the "from 01.01.2027" notes where the rules are in force and applying, checking whether they were amended before commencement.
6. Update the version and date in the `SKILL.md` header, in the README and in the changelog below.
